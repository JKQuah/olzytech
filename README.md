## About us

Too many businesses end up juggling a designer, a developer and a hosting provider who never talk to each other. Olzytech was founded to fix that: one team from the very first requirement through to deployment and beyond.

We are a small, senior team. We work with individuals, startups, SMEs and established brands in Malaysia, Singapore and Hong Kong (and remotely with clients worldwide), and we treat every project as if it were our own product: measurable goals, honest timelines and no jargon.

## What we do

- **Website design:** distinctive UI/UX, design systems and brand-aligned visuals that look sharp on every screen and turn visitors into customers.
- **Website development:** fast, accessible, SEO-ready sites and web apps built with modern frameworks and clean, maintainable code.
- **Requirements and planning:** your idea turned into a clear scope, sitemap, user flows and timeline, so there are no surprises later.
- **Deployment and hosting:** CI/CD pipelines, domains, SSL, monitoring and cloud hosting, set up properly and handed over with documentation.
- **SEO and performance:** technical SEO, Core Web Vitals tuning, structured data and analytics, so people can find you and stay.
- **Care and maintenance:** updates, backups, security patches and ongoing improvements after launch. We stay on as your web team.

## How we work

1. **Discover:** we workshop your goals, audience and requirements, then deliver the scope, a sitemap and a fixed timeline.
2. **Design:** wireframes, visual direction and a clickable prototype you can react to before any code is written.
3. **Build:** component-driven development, CMS integration and content migration, demoed every sprint.
4. **Launch:** QA, performance and accessibility checks, then a zero-downtime deployment to production.
5. **Grow:** analytics, SEO and continuous improvement to keep your site ahead as your business grows.

## What we believe

- **AI is a tool, not the creator.** We use AI to move faster, then people design, review and decide. Speed for you, accountability with us.
- **Innovation is gold.** We keep exploring better ideas and modern technology, so your business stands out instead of blending in.
- **Performance is a feature.** Fast pages, accessible interfaces and solid SEO are part of the definition of done.
- **Partners, not vendors.** Clear scope, honest progress, and we stay close after launch to grow with your business.
